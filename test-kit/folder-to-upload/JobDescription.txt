Consultant / Developer (fictional posting for testing)

A software company that builds financial reporting products for law firms
is looking for a Consultant/Developer. You will work directly with client
firms to implement our products: understanding each firm's needs,
translating them into technical requirements, and delivering them, from
implementation through post-live support.

What you'll do
- Manage client implementations and projects to successful outcomes.
- Work closely with our consultants to implement, customize and support
  our products.
- Design and build reports and dashboards.
- Design, code, deploy and test client-specific customizations.
- Carry out technical investigations.
- Provide full life-cycle client support.

What we're looking for
- T-SQL knowledge is required at a minimum. Our stack is SQL Server
  (T-SQL, SSAS, SSRS), HTML5, AngularJS and C#. We will consider strong
  developers familiar with other technologies: strong developers can learn
  any stack.
- Core consultancy skills: strong communication and people skills.
- Prior experience in a similar customer-facing technical role.
- Experience in legal IT is a big plus, but not required.


--- Screening notes ---

Dealbreakers:
1. Knowledge of T-SQL (required at a minimum).
2. Prior experience in a similar customer-facing technical role.
3. Strong development skills (strong developers can learn any stack).

What success looks like in 6-12 months:
Managing client implementations to successful outcomes: gathering
requirements, designing and building reports, dashboards and client-specific
customizations, and supporting clients through the full project lifecycle,
including post-live support.

What kind of person thrives here:
A developer with a customer-centric mindset; motivated, a creative problem
solver, able to translate a client's needs into technical requirements and
communicate well with clients and consultants.
